Intro
- Goals:
-- Introduce player to the world and intial set of characters
-- Teach player basics of card game and give them initial cards
-- Show a couple puzzles to communicate the world is alive
- Emotion:
-- Atmosphere should feel charming
-- Sense of charm / quirkiness through characters and dialog
-- Get a sense that there are many more and stronger cards
-- Feel a desire to explore this new world
- Events:
-- Arrive on train, met at station by brother, reuniting after a while
-- Brother takes player back to homestead (little out of town in rural area), meet wife and father
-- Learn about Card Game
-- Learn about upcoming card game festival in lake town
-- Learn that player is perhaps there because of some tragedy and they are getting away / starting anew - from convo with brother

Early Game
- Goals:
-- Player gets a couple concrete goals to drive story forward
-- Player gets to explore the first town, end learns there are others
-- Main mystery is presented, player sees vision of figure related to tragedy
- Emotion:
-- Player experiences the world has become bigger and sees there are plenty of things to do
-- Recognize that the world itself has some mystery to it
-- Feel a sense of excitement for collecting cards and defeating players in the card game
-- Should feel like card game is the way conflicts are settled in the world
- Events:
-- Does multiple quests in town
-- Player introduced other recurring characters, Dez & Jord, other rural families, quircky town folk
-- Enters festival tournament, learns of multiple towns and other tournaments, wants to become the best
-- Sees vision of character from past, player compelled to explore world to investigate
