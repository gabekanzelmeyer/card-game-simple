card abilities
- shield: block one attack regardless of amount, shield is broken
- regenerate: on death return to hand
- quickstep: attacks before other card
- timebound: all cards with timebound must be played before any other cards in hand
- terrain X: set terrain to boost all cards of that color +X/+X)

- fortify X: give random hand card +X health
- fortress X: give all hand cards +X health
- warcry X: give random hand card +X attack
- warmonger X: give all hand cards +X attack
- headshot X: dead X damage to opposing card
- popshot X: deal X damage to random opp hand card
- shotgun X: deal X damage to all opp hand cards
- sacrifice X: discard X cards form hand
- assasinate X: destrot X cards in opponenets hand
- disarm X: reduce opposite card attack by X
- blunt X: reduce random opp hand card attack by X
- cripple X: reduce all opp hand card attack by X
- enchant [ability]: gives ability to random card in hand
- bless [ability]: gives ability to all cards in hand
- curse [ability]: gives ability to random card in opp hand
- prism [color] X: all cards of color get +X/+X
- dim [color] X: all cards of color get -X/-X
